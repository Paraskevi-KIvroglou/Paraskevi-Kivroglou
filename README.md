# Hey, I'm Paraskevi 👋

I'm an AI engineer with a mathematics background. I founded **AureliaEdge**, where we build AI agents, workflow automation and AI search visibility for growing businesses. Before that: AI consulting in London, C# / .NET / Azure engineering in Germany, and product work at wiserwork.ai.

I like technology that is useful on a Tuesday afternoon, not just impressive in a demo. Most of what I build starts with one slow workflow and ends with a system the team keeps running on its own.

AureliaEdge is an [OpenAI Select Partner](https://www.theaureliaedge.com/about#openai-partner) in the OpenAI Partner Network. We work with businesses in the US, Canada, the UK and the EU. The model is simple: a free audit, then one workflow that pays for itself, then expand only when the numbers hold.

- **Speed to lead.** Every enquiry captured, qualified, routed and answered in minutes, with the CRM updated automatically.
- **AEO + SEO.** Pages that AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity can quote correctly, and that rank in Google.
- **AI agents and automation.** Inbox triage, drafting, research, reporting and handoffs, measured against a baseline before anything expands.
